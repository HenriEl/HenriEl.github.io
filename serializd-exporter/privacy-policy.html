Privacy Policy — Serializd Data Extractor by Bingeboxd

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Serializd Data Extractor by Bingeboxd (“the extension”) is a browser tool that lets you export your own Serializd data to a CSV file on your computer. This policy explains what it does and doesn’t do with your information.

Summary

The extension runs entirely in your browser. It does not send your data to us or to any third-party server, it does not store your data, and it does not track you. There are no analytics and no accounts.

What the extension accesses

When you click Export to CSV while signed in to serializd.com, the extension:

  1. Reads your signed-in Serializd session token from the Serializd page so it can request your data on your behalf.
  2. Calls Serializd’s own endpoints (the same ones the Serializd website uses) to retrieve your watched episodes, ratings, reviews, watchlist and paused/dropped shows.
  3. Converts that data into a CSV file in memory.
  4. Triggers a download of that CSV to your device.

That is the full extent of its data handling.

What we collect

Nothing. The extension has no backend server. Your Serializd data and session token are used only locally, in your browser, for the duration of the export. None of it is transmitted to the developer or any other party, and none of it is retained by the extension after the export finishes.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • Host access to serializd.com — to read your data from your logged-in Serializd session.
  • scripting / activeTab — to run the export logic in the Serializd tab you have open.
  • downloads — to save the resulting CSV file to your device.

Data sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer any user data. We never receive it in the first place.

Third parties

The extension communicates only with Serializd’s servers, using your own session, to retrieve your data. It is an unofficial tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Serializd or its owners. “Serializd” is a trademark of its respective owner.

Your control

The extension only does anything when you click Export to CSV. You can remove it at any time from chrome://extensions, which ends all access immediately.
